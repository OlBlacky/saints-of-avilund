# Languages

The tongues of Avilund, their Families, and how characters learn them. The
code's source of truth is `app/src/lib/languages.ts`; this is the design
record. For the Skill/Language split see design-principles §10 ("Skills know,
Languages read").

## The rules

- **Every character speaks two tongues free from creation: Imperial**, the
  common tongue, **and their home language** — the Dialect or standalone
  vernacular of where they grew up, chosen at creation.
- **Every further language is its own buy: 1 Minor Advance.** Classes and
  Subclasses grant some outright (the Scholar and Friar grant Auld Imperial;
  casting Subclasses grant their tradition's tongue).
- **Language Families.** The mundane vernaculars group into three Families —
  **Imperial**, **Republic**, **Regnal** — one per bloc of the map. Each
  region speaks a **Dialect**, named with its Family in front
  ("Imperial - Lysandrine"). A speaker of one Dialect can make themselves
  understood in another Dialect of the same Family at **−1 to social checks**.
  Across Families, or with a tongue outside every Family, there is no such
  kinship.

## The vernaculars

| Family | Dialect | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Imperial | — (the koiné itself) | everywhere; the Empire's common tongue |
| Imperial | Imperial - Lysandrine | the Bishopric of St. Dunstan and Lysander; salted with Western loanwords from the crusader intermarriage |
| Imperial | Imperial - Severic | the Bishoprics of St. Severinius and St. Mairin |
| Imperial | Imperial - Vailish | Valdenwail; the court dialect, the "purest" Imperial |
| Republic | Republic - Waldisch | Waldheim |
| Republic | Republic - Moortongue | Dunstanmoore; Havilah speaks a drifted colonial variant |
| Republic | Republic - Veldish | Isenveld |
| Regnal | Regnal - Patric | Patrilund |
| Regnal | Regnal - Mantlish | Mantlethorn |
| Regnal | Regnal - Heirgallic | Heirgallad |

Standing alone, outside every Family:

- **Cyprian** — the tongue of the Bishopric of St. Ignatius, with its own
  alphabet, devised by St. Cyprian in the early Repurgo to hide the
  Bishopric's books from the burners. Taught in every Ignatian grammar school.
- **Kellish** — the tongue of the Kells; the living animist vernacular,
  deeply infused with First Tongue. Its blood-ties to the North grant no
  Dialect kinship.
- **Common Feral** — the Feral lingua franca; mostly spoken, little writing.
  The Ferals hold many tongues; the rest are unnamed for now.

## The learned, dead, and magic tongues

- **Auld Imperial** — the Church's and scholarship's working language; child
  of Elder and the Archipelago tongue. Imperial descends from it in turn,
  which is why the koiné is the koiné.
- **Archipelago** — the Holy Islands' ancestral tongue; nearly extinct; the
  faith's original sacred speech.
- **Elder** — the extinct Elders' spoken tongue; nearly extinct.
- **Elder Arcana Tongue** — the Elders' arcane notation; Elder Magic is
  written in it.
- **Arcane Tongue** — the modern arcane notation, child of the Elder Arcana
  Tongue; New Magic is written in it.
- **First Tongue** — the ur-language, ancestor of all speech; survives as
  rock-carvings, scattered phrases, and its infusion in Kellish. The tongue of
  Old Magic.
- **Black Tongue** — the secret tongue of the Black Faith; curses and the
  Occult.
- **Eldritch Tongue** — the speech of the Outside.

## Open

- The Westerners' tongue is deliberately unnamed (Les will reach the West
  much later); only its loanwords in Lysandrine are canon.
- The individual Feral tongues beyond Common Feral.

# Companions

A **Companion** is a creature that belongs to a character: a bonded beast, a bound shade, a hired man, a familiar. They are wildly different things at the table, and they all run on **one chassis** — the same card shape, the same stat block, the same advancement machinery — with a **Type** that sets a handful of dials.

This is the general rule set. Each Companion card states its Type and inherits everything below.

---

## The chassis — what every Companion has

| Piece | Rule |
|---|---|
| **A Type** | Beast, Summoned, and the Types reserved below. The Type sets the five dials. |
| **A card** | Every Companion comes from an Ability card, bought for 1 Major like any Ability. **The card is the bond, not the beast** — see Replacement. |
| **A name and a description** | The player's to write, on every Companion without exception. |
| **A stat block** | HP · Speed · AC and other Defences · DC · an attack line · whatever senses or skills it is named for. Companion stat blocks are the first creature stat blocks in the game. |
| **Ladders** | The card's advancement track, where the Type has one. A Companion Ladder climbs **≤1 Rank per Level**, like every other Ladder. |
| **Orders** | What it will do when told. Every Companion understands a vocabulary of **Orders**; the Type says how they are given, what they cost, and how the list grows. A beast is taught them; a bound thing is given them; a hired man is asked. |

A Companion is **not a character**. It has no attributes, no Class, no Skills, no Feats, and no Abilities of its own — only the lines its stat block names and the Ladders on its card. Its Defences serve as its Saves. It rolls nothing the block does not give it.

A card grants **one** Companion at a time.

---

## The five dials

The Type answers five questions, and those answers are the whole difference between a sheepdog and a shade.

| Dial | The question |
|---|---|
| **Command** | How does it act — its own turn, or the owner's action? What will it do at all? |
| **Permanence** | Does it stay until it dies, or expire on a Duration? |
| **Loyalty** | Can it refuse? |
| **Upkeep** | What does keeping it cost? |
| **Numbers** | Whose are they — its own, climbing its own Level, or the card's and the owner's? |

| Dial | **Beast** | **Summoned** |
|---|---|---|
| Command | Its own turn, its own initiative. Orders are **free**. | It acts **only** when the owner spends the Action the card names. |
| Permanence | Until it dies. | The card's **Duration**, then it is gone. |
| Loyalty | Cannot refuse, but knows only its **Orders**. | Cannot refuse. It is bound. |
| Upkeep | Feed, **1 cp/day**. | A material component, spent per summoning. |
| Numbers | Its own stat block, climbing its **own Level**. | Stated by the card, climbing the **card's Ladders**. |

## Beast — the Animal Companion

A living animal, bonded to its owner. The first is the Naturalist's **Shepherd's Dog** (Husbandry).

- **Command.** It acts on its own turn, on its own initiative. Directing it costs the owner nothing — but it will do only what it has been taught. **Orders are its command vocabulary**: an Order is a standing instruction it will carry out (*Come, Stay, Attack, Guard, Fetch, Follow*), and it knows as many as its Orders Ladder allows. Ask it for anything outside its Orders and it does not understand you.
- **Permanence.** Bonding takes **weeks** of training — days, with the Naturalist's Beast-Wise. Once bonded it stays until it dies.
- **Loyalty.** No Morale check. A bonded beast is loyal; the limit on it is its Orders, not its nerve. It takes Conditions like any creature, and a Frightened beast behaves as the Condition says.
- **Upkeep.** It eats: **1 cp/day**, foraged or fed. A beast left unfed for a week leaves — treat that as death for the Advance rules below.
- **Numbers.** Its own, and it grows:
  - **Its own Level** — **0** when bonded, rising whenever its owner gains a Level *thereafter*. One taken late, or a replacement, lags behind for good.
  - **Its DC is 10 + its Level**, like any creature's.
  - **Its own earn rate is m–m–M** — each of its Levels earns it **1 Minor Advance**, and every third Level a **Major**. Deliberately slimmer than a character's curve: the beast stays a beast.
  - **The owner may spend his own Advances** on its Ladders too, under normal pacing.

## Summoned

A thing called up and held for a while. The first — and, for now, the only one — is the Witch's **Bind Spirit** (Witchcraft).

- **Command.** It does nothing on its own. The owner spends the Action the card names to command it, and that action cost is a Ladder on the card: buying it down from a Move Action to a Minor is exactly the purchase Rail 6 prices as a Major.
- **Permanence.** It lasts the card's **Duration** and then it is gone. Nothing carries over to the next summoning.
- **Loyalty.** It cannot refuse. The binding is the loyalty, and the component is the tether.
- **Upkeep.** No wage and no feed — a **material component consumed per summoning** (Bind Spirit: 1 sp of powdered obsidian).
- **Numbers.** Not its own. The card states them and the card's Ladders raise them, bought with the **owner's** Advances like any Ability. A summoned thing has **no Level of its own** and earns no Advances; **its DC is its owner's**. At **0 HP the binding breaks** and it is gone — it does not fall Unconscious, it does not lie Dying, and nothing is refunded, because nothing was invested in the creature.

## Death, and the Advances inside it

This is the rule the whole framework turns on, and it applies to any Companion that has a Level of its own.

- A Companion with its own HP **falls Unconscious at 0 and dies at −10**, on the ordinary rules for [falling, dying, and death](../app/src/content/rules/combat.md). An ally who reaches it in time keeps it alive; it cannot self-stabilize.
- **When it dies, the owner's invested Advances return to him** — respendable on himself or on a new Companion.
- **The Companion's own earned Advances die with it.** They were the beast's, not the owner's.
- **Replacement.** The card is the bond, not the beast: the owner does not pay the Major again. A new Companion starts at **Level 0** and takes the Type's bonding time.

Companions never earn Milestones. Every Level a Companion has is derived from its owner's.

## Types reserved

Named here so the dials are already waiting for them; the rules are not written yet.

- **Henchman** — a hired person. The one Type where **Loyalty** does real work: the five-rung **Hireling Morale** ladder (Broken → Wavering → Steady → Resolute → Devoted) already exists in [friar.md](characters/friar.md), and wages already exist in the economy. A henchman is a person, so his numbers will be a person's.
- **Familiar** — a caster's small bound creature. The Type that will test the **Numbers** dial: derived from its master rather than climbing a Level of its own.
- **Mount** — a beast whose rules are mostly about carrying and moving another creature.

## Open

- **Order effects.** A Beast's Orders are named (*Come, Stay, Attack, Guard, Fetch, Follow*) and counted by the Orders Ladder, but what each one does at the table is unwritten.
